Frequently asked questions

What this site is, who it is for, and how it pays for itself.

Who is this site for?

Adults over 30 with full-time jobs, families and unpredictable weeks — people who want to be healthier without rebuilding their entire life around it.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. Almost every workout guide here is built around bodyweight movement and a mat. Where equipment helps, it is a resistance band or a set of adjustable dumbbells, never a gym membership.

How long are the routines?

Most are 10 to 30 minutes. The whole premise of the site is that a short session you actually do beats a long one you skip.

Is the advice medical advice?

No. Everything here is general information for healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are returning from injury, talk to a doctor before changing how you train or eat.

How do you make money?

Through affiliate links, mainly Amazon. If you buy something through a link on this site we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what gets recommended.

How often do you publish?

New guides go up regularly across five areas: movement, nutrition, sleep, supplements and habit systems.
